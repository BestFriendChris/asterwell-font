The reading room kept its own weather. Rain moved along the tall
windows in slow diagonals and the radiators ticked, patient as
something waiting to be noticed. Mara set the proof sheet down, read
the opening line again — “Every letter is an argument about how long
you will stay” — and drew a small star in the margin beside it.✽

She had been told once that a page is only paper until somebody slows
down for it. At nine in the morning that sounded sentimental. By four,
with the day’s light gone amber and the stack still unfinished, it
sounded exactly right: the shapes were doing the work of a voice,
holding the sentence open, letting it breathe… It is a quiet claim,
and this page is where it is tested.
